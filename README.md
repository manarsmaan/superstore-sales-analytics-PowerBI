# 📊 Superstore Sales & Financial Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Executive Summary
An interactive two-page Power BI dashboard designed to evaluate retail sales performance, profit margins, customer trends, and logistics efficiency. The report transforms raw transaction data into actionable business insights to identify revenue drivers and mitigate profit leakage.

---

## 📈 Key Metrics & Highlights
* **Total Sales:** $2.3M across 5K+ completed orders.
* **Total Profit:** $286K with an overall **12.5%** profit margin.
* **Customer Base:** 793 active customers with an **Average Order Value (AOV)** of $458.60.
* **Top Category:** Technology leads total revenue at **$836K (36.4%)**, followed by Office Supplies ($742K) and Furniture ($719K).

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights
1. **Q4 Seasonality Peak:** Monthly sales ramp up significantly in September and peak during November–December.
2. **Fulfillment Distribution:** Standard Class is the preferred shipping method (**60.19%** of orders).
3. **Profit Leakage:** Heavy discounting on **Tables** and **Bookcases** generates net negative margins despite strong gross sales.
